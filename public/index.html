Privacy

  • No wallet connection. You paste a public address. Nothing to sign, no access to your funds.
  • We keep simple stats so we can improve Kickback: the addresses checked, what was found, the X handle if you enter one, country and the site you came from.
  • No IP addresses, no ad cookies, no tracking across sites.
  • Cards you post or copy are saved so the share link works.
  • Want something removed? DM @syanovee on X.

Disclaimer

  • Not financial advice. Kickback only shows what a wallet has already received. It doesn't tell you what to buy, hold or sell.
  • Numbers can be off. Values use today's prices, not the price on the day of each payout. Some rewards may be missing, for example very old ones or ones we can't verify on-chain yet.
  • Read-only. We only read public blockchain data. No wallet connection, nothing to sign, no access to funds.
  • Not affiliated with StonkFun, pump.fun, Flap, Pons, Ember, Four.meme or any other platform. Names and logos belong to their owners.
  • No token. Kickback has no coin and no CA. Anyone offering one is not us.
