···How it works·

Privacy

  • You paste a public address. There's nothing to sign and we never touch your funds.
  • We log the addresses checked, wallets we found linked to them through bridges, what we found, your X handle if you add one, your country and the site that sent you.
  • To keep checks fast we keep the Solana transactions we read for a wallet for 45 days, and the last full result for each wallet. Anyone who checks the same wallet sees that saved result, the same as a fresh check would show.
  • No IP addresses stored, no ad cookies, no tracking across sites.
  • Cards you post or copy are saved so the link keeps working. Logs, saved results and cards have no set expiry yet.
  • Want a wallet removed or kept off the homepage? DM @syanovee on X.

Disclaimer

  • This isn't financial advice. Kickback shows what a wallet already got, not what to buy or sell.
  • Totals use today's prices, not the price on the day of each payout. Very old rewards, or ones we can't verify on-chain yet, may be missing.
  • We only read public blockchain data. No wallet connection, nothing to sign.
  • We're not affiliated with StonkFun, pump.fun, Flap, Pons, Ember, Four.meme or any other platform. Names and logos belong to their owners.
  • Kickback has no token and no CA. If someone is selling one, it's not us.
  • getkickback.fun is our only site. We never DM links and never ask for a seed phrase or private key.
